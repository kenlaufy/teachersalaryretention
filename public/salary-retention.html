Massachusetts school districts · DESE data

Do Massachusetts districts that pay teachers more keep more of them?

Slightly. Higher-paying districts tend to keep a bit more of their teachers, but pay explains little of the difference, and most of the gap is between the lowest-paying quarter of districts and the rest.

        Source

        Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE), School and District Profiles: Teacher Salaries report (2023–24 school year) and Staffing Retention Rates report (2025). Retrieved October 15, 2026.

        Coverage

        393 districts with both an average teacher salary and a teacher retention rate. State totals and 6 districts missing salary or retention data were excluded. Teacher FTE = full-time-equivalent teachers in the salary report.

        Method

        Unweighted least-squares fit of retention on average salary, one point per district. Quartiles use R's default (type 7) cut points. Charter status is read from the district name, so it is approximate.
